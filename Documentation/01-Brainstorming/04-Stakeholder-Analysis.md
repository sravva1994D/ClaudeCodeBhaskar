## 2. Governance (2026-11-10, OQ-026)

- Clinic Owner = General Physician = Product Owner = Project Owner (one person)
- Single decision-maker
- No stakeholder escalation process required
- No approval workflow beyond the Project Owner

## 3. Secondary Users

None. The BRD states receptionist access is excluded from Phase 1 (OOS-001). No other user role is defined.

## 4. Stakeholder Observations (resolved)

- **STK-001 and STK-002:** the same person. Resolved by OQ-026 (2026-11-10).
- **Product Owner identity:** the same person as the General Physician. Resolved by OQ-026.
- **Patients:** data subjects, not direct users. Retention and consent are deferred (OQ-017; OOS-015).
- **Regulators and insurers:** not named in the BRD. Insurance processing is out of scope (OOS-003). Regulatory scope is deferred (OQ-017).
